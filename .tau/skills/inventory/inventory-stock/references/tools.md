# Available MCP tools

Confirm the exact schema with `erp_tools_describe`. The current catalogue reflects access and module readiness.

- `inventory_balances_get` — Read a record in balances by UID.
- `inventory_balances_list` — Find stock balances.
- `inventory_locations_archive` — Archive an eligible locations record at its expected version.
- `inventory_locations_create` — Create a stock location.
- `inventory_locations_get` — Read a record in locations by UID.
- `inventory_locations_list` — Find stock locations.
- `inventory_locations_restore` — Restore an eligible locations record at its expected version.
- `inventory_locations_update` — Edit a location at its expected version.
- `inventory_movements_cancel` — Cancel an eligible stock movement at its expected version.
- `inventory_movements_complete` — Complete an eligible stock movement at its expected version.
- `inventory_movements_create` — Create a draft owned stock movement.
- `inventory_movements_create_from_sales` — Create a draft delivery linked to a confirmed Sales order.
- `inventory_movements_get` — Read a record in movements by UID.
- `inventory_movements_list` — Find stock movements.
- `inventory_movements_prepare` — Prepare an eligible stock movement at its expected version.
- `inventory_stock_items_archive` — Archive an eligible stock-items record at its expected version.
- `inventory_stock_items_create` — Create a stock item policy for a Catalog product.
- `inventory_stock_items_get` — Read a record in stock-items by UID.
- `inventory_stock_items_list` — Find stock stock-items.
- `inventory_stock_items_restore` — Restore an eligible stock-items record at its expected version.
