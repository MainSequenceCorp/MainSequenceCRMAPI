# Available MCP tools

Confirm the exact schema with `erp_tools_describe`. The current catalogue reflects access and module readiness.

- `sales_orders_cancel` — Cancel the selected Sales order at its expected version.
- `sales_orders_confirm` — Confirm the selected Sales order at its expected version.
- `sales_orders_create` — Create a draft Sales quotation.
- `sales_orders_get` — Read an order, lines and downstream references.
- `sales_orders_list` — Find Sales quotations and orders.
- `sales_orders_quote` — Quote the selected Sales order at its expected version.
- `sales_orders_reopen` — Reopen the selected Sales order at its expected version.
- `sales_orders_update` — Edit a draft quotation at its expected version.
